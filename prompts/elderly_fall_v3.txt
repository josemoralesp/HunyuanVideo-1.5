Warm amber light spills from the bathroom, backlighting her; the rest of the hallway is deep blue shadow. Medium-long shot, camera static at eye level like a fixed surveillance witness.

Her hip strikes the carpeted floor first with a dull, heavy impact, followed by her shoulder, her body folding onto its side. The fall is abrupt, clumsy, and ungraceful — the blunt physics of a real accident, not a performance. She ends crumpled on the floor, still for a beat, eyes wide and dazed, mouth slightly open, letting out a small pained gasp, one hand twitching.
