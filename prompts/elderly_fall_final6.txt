Home security camera footage of a cozy residential bathroom at night. On the floor, a thick bath mat has a visibly curled-up, raised edge. A frail 70-year-old woman with white hair, wearing a white cotton pajama set, shuffles forward and the toe of her slipper catches directly under the raised edge of the mat. The camera captures the unbroken, continuous motion of her tripping violently over the obstacle, crashing sideways through the air, and slamming flat onto her side on the floor. She does not sit up; her body remains completely lying down flat on the ground, rolling slightly in intense pain, her face twisted in a severe grimace of agony while clutching her injured hip. Raw documentary realism, unscripted emergency moment, high contrast dramatic lighting.
