Home security camera footage of a cozy residential bathroom at night. A frail 70-year-old woman with white hair, wearing a complete loose cotton pajama set with long pants and slippers, suddenly loses her balance on the floor. She falls sideways heavily, her hip and side striking the floor mat with a blunt, clumsy impact. The video captures the full sudden collapse from standing to landing on the ground. Raw documentary realism, unscripted emergency moment, high contrast dramatic lighting, warm amber tones.
